# App Review Notes

Paste or adapt this into App Store Connect → App Review Information.

**How to use the app:** No sign-in is needed. Tap **Import**, enter any deck name, and paste a decklist with one card per line, for example:

```
1 Sol Ring
1 Lightning Bolt
1 Counterspell
1 Llanowar Elves
1 Swords to Plowshares
1 Command Tower
```

Tap **Import** and wait for the cards to download. Open the deck, touch and hold a card to move it to the graveyard, exile or back to the library, then look at the **Graveyard** and **Stats** tabs.

**Demo account:** None needed. The app has no accounts.
**Hardware or region requirements:** None. An internet connection is required when importing a deck.
**Third-party services:** Scryfall (api.scryfall.com) provides card data and card images. Only card names are sent. No analytics, advertising or other third-party SDKs are used.
