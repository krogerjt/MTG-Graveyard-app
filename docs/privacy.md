# Privacy Policy — GraveyardTracker

**Effective date:** 10/07/2026
**Publisher:** Jacob Kroger  
**Contact:** [jakekroger@gmail.com](mailto:jakekroger@gmail.com)

## Summary

Graveyard Tracker does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The publisher does not receive, store or have access to your decks or any other data from the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and it does not use your location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

## Data stored on your device

Decks you import (deck name, optional commander, and card lists), card details and card images downloaded for them, and the state of games you track are stored in a local database inside the app on your device. This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete a deck at any time by swiping it in the deck list, and deleting the app removes all of its data. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or computer backups), which are controlled by you and Apple.

## Network use and third-party services

When you import a deck or look up a card, the app sends the card names from your decklist to the Scryfall card database service (api.scryfall.com) over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and downloads card images from image addresses that Scryfall provides. Your deck name and commander field are not sent. As with any internet request, Scryfall can see your device's IP address and that a request came from the app (which identifies itself as "GraveyardTracker/1.0"). Scryfall is an independent service not operated by the publisher, and its own terms and privacy policy apply to the requests it receives. Refer to the Scryfall and Wizards of the Coast policies:

- [Scryfall API documentation](https://scryfall.com/docs/api)
- [Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy)

## Children

Graveyard Tracker is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices and rights

All of your data is stored on your own device, so you control it directly: you can view it in the app, delete any deck by swiping it in the deck list, and remove everything by deleting the app. Because the publisher does not collect or hold your data, there is nothing for the publisher to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Privacy laws in some regions, such as the EU, the UK and California, give people rights over personal data that an organization holds. If you have a privacy question or request, contact the publisher at the email address above. Requests about the data Scryfall receives should be directed to Scryfall.

## Changes to this policy

Changes to this policy will be referenced in the App Store release notes, and the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
